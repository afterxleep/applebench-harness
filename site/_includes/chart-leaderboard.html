{%- comment -%} Every published run, ranked by the share of available points it earned. One measure, one series · magnitude by length, so a single hue and no legend. Share rather than raw points, because runs are scored against different numbers of tasks as the suite grows, and a raw total would rank a model on how much of the suite it happened to be measured against. The task count rides along on every bar for the same reason: two runs on different task sets are not the same measurement, and the chart has to say so rather than imply otherwise.
