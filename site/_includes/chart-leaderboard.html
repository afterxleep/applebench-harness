Pass rate and points are not the same number. Pass rate is how many tasks reached a verified result. Points start from those same solves and then fall when a task spent more than the 50,000-token allowance, floored at 25%. A model can finish more of the suite and still sit close on points if the extra solves were expensive. Pass rate is the first reading; points and cost say what those passes cost to get.
